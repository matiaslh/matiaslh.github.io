# About

I'm a software engineer at Meta in New York, in the Applied AI org. I build hard, expertise-heavy tasks that frontier models struggle to solve, and research scientists use them to benchmark and improve the models.

Right now that means shipping production-ready games in Godot with my team, for web, native and macOS. Before that I built multi-turn terminal tasks, where every extra step compounds the difficulty.

Before Applied AI, I spent almost four years at Meta Reality Labs in Seattle working on VR calling, real-time avatar pipelines and the Horizon Media Player on Quest. Before that I was at AWS on the DocumentDB team. I studied Computer Science at the University of Guelph.
